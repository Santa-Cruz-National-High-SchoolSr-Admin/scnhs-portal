Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

At Santa Cruz National High School (SCNHS), we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect the data you provide to us through our website and portals.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information such as your name, Learner Reference Number (LRN), email address, contact number, and academic records when you use our student or admin portals, or when you submit an online registration.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used for educational and administrative purposes, including but not limited to:

  • Processing your enrollment and academic records.
  • Providing access to student and admin portals.
  • Communicating important school updates, news, and announcements.
  • Improving our website and services.

3. Data Protection and Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to safeguard your personal data against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. Access to personal data is restricted to authorized school personnel only.

4. Sharing of Information

SCNHS does not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information with government agencies such as DepEd as required by law for educational compliance.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction, or deletion of your personal data stored in our systems. For any privacy-related concerns, please contact the ICT Department or the Registrar's Office.
